What to check

  • Over-trimming. If a painting has a pale passage running to its edge, the trim may have eaten it. Compare against the original dimensions shown under each card.
  • Under-trimming. A visible white border means the threshold needs raising for that asset.
  • Aspect ratio. It should match the painting's real proportions — that ratio becomes permanent content-model data.
  • Mapping. Sections marked ⚠ have slugs that contradict their own rendered titles. The artist must confirm which works belong where.
