# Where these came from, and what is settled

## Sources

**The artist's own site**, karimabdelmalak.com. Images fetched from the
transform-free path on its CDN, which returns the stored original. The
photograph of the artist in the studio and the large landscape painting came
from here at full size.

**Safarkhan Art Gallery**, safarkhan.com, who have represented him since 2016.
The catalogue records for Wesal (2025) and Zat (2023) come from their exhibition
pages: titles, years, media, dimensions and sale status, with photography up to
8587px. Nothing comparable exists on the artist's own site.

**The artist's YouTube channel**, for the three videos.

## Permissions, unresolved

The photography from Safarkhan is very likely the gallery's own, and the
curatorial texts on their site certainly are. **Using these images on the
artist's website has not been confirmed with him or with the gallery.**

That confirmation is a short conversation, not a hard problem: galleries
normally supply their artists with images of their own work. But it has not
happened yet, so treat these as prepared, not cleared. No curatorial text has
been copied anywhere in this package.

The CV, exhibition history and press list are the artist's own facts, recovered
from his own site.
